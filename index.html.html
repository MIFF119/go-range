VR SANDBOX SHOOTING RANGE · BUILT FOR THE OCULUS GO

IN VR (OCULUS GO)

  • Trigger = shoot
  • Flick controller down + back up = reload (mag swap!)
  • Thumb on touchpad (no click) = walk
  • Click touchpad centre = next gun · bottom = reload · left/right edge = turn
  • Board on your left: shoot buttons to spawn stuff + NPCs
  • Mannequins: leg = limp, both legs = crawl, arm = they hold it, head = down
  • WEB (board): hold trigger to yank things or zip yourself to walls · ROPE: click 2 points to tie them
  • Turn around: there's a back room behind you

ON A LAPTOP (FOR TESTING)

  • Click to lock the mouse, then click = shoot
  • Hold click = full auto (rifle)
  • WASD = walk · Shift = sprint
  • R or E = reload · 1 2 3 or Q = guns
  • 4 = web · 5 = rope · C = cut all webs
  • Esc = free your mouse
