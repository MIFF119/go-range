VR SANDBOX SHOOTING RANGE · BUILT FOR THE OCULUS GO

IN VR (OCULUS GO)

  • You start with a HAND. Point + hold trigger = grab (guns, people, crates...). Swing + let go = throw
  • Weapons table on your right: grab a gun, RPG, knife, axe, sword, nade or rock
  • Click touchpad centre = drop what you're holding (empty hand: hand / web / rope)
  • Trigger = shoot · flick down + up or click pad bottom = reload · pad edges = turn
  • Thumb on touchpad (no click) = walk
  • Blades: swing your wrist (or pull trigger) to chop. Fast swings cut limbs off
  • Nades + rocks: hold trigger, swing, let go. Rocks knock people out
  • Mannequins have personalities: shoot near them and they fight, run, crouch or freeze. Fighters hit back: watch your HP bar
  • Give a weapon: point at a mannequin up close + click pad centre. They become your buddy and fight for you
  • Human shield: empty hand, walk right up to someone + trigger. You hold them in front and pull a pistol. Pad centre = shove them away
  • The bad guy house (end of the street, red bandanas): armed gang + a cash stash. They warn you once
  • Rob the shops: aim a gun at the dairy owner or the BUN RUN worker. They hand over cash, refuse, or fight. Refused? Warning shots or shoot their hand
  • Board on your left: shoot or point + trigger to spawn stuff
  • Walk right past the table: the town (burger joint, dairy, house). Back room is behind you

ON A LAPTOP (FOR TESTING)

  • Click to lock the mouse, then click = use (shoot / grab / chop)
  • Hold click = full auto rifle, or hold things with the hand
  • WASD = walk · Shift = sprint
  • G = drop (aim at a mannequin = give it to them) · R or E = reload · Q = hand / web / rope
  • 1 = hand · 4 = web · 5 = rope · C = cut all webs
  • Esc = free your mouse
